# NyaySetu

NyaySetu is a civic accountability platform engineered for Jalandhar that bridges private citizen grievance resolution with public community petition momentum.

## Links

- **Live Platform**: [nyaysetu-c8c4.vercel.app](https://nyaysetu-c8c4.vercel.app/)
- **Source Code**: [github.com/anuptiwari17/nyaysetu](https://github.com/anuptiwari17/nyaysetu)

## Overview

Civic issue resolution often suffers from opaque tracking and lack of department accountability. NyaySetu introduces a dual-channel architecture: citizens submit private grievances directly to municipal authorities, and if unresolved, escalate them into public petitions to mobilize community support.

## Key Features

- **Private Grievance Desk**: Direct, secure issue reporting routed to municipal departments with status milestones (Assigned, In Progress, Resolved).
- **Public Petition Escalation**: Community campaign layer allowing citizens to sign public petitions and drive collective visibility.
- **Department Routing**: Automated grievance categorization for municipal units including Water Supply, PWD, Sanitation, and Electricity.
- **Proof-Based Resolution**: Transparent resolution tracking requiring authorities to upload resolution proof before closing cases.
- **Impact Metrics**: Real-time petition tracking and supporter verification counters.

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons
- **Backend Services**: Node.js, REST APIs
- **Database & Auth**: PostgreSQL, Secure Authentication
- **Hosting**: Vercel
